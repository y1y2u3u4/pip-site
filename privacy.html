Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Pip is a study app. We process only what the features need. No accounts, no ads, no cross-app tracking, and we never sell data.

On your iPhone

Your PDFs, study kits, plans, answers, progress and settings are stored on your iPhone, in Pip's app storage. Deleting a PDF in Pip deletes everything made from it; “Erase all data” in Settings removes everything.

AI features (only with your consent)

Before anything leaves your phone, Pip asks for your permission. After that:

  • Building a study kit sends the text of the pages you choose (never the PDF file) to our server.
  • Ask your notes sends your question and the text of up to three relevant pages.

Our server passes this text to Anthropic's Claude model through OpenRouter to write guides, flashcards, questions and answers, then returns the result. We don't store your notes, questions or answers on our server. Anthropic and OpenRouter process the text under their own privacy policies and may keep it for a limited time for abuse prevention. You can turn AI off at any time in Settings; the sample notes and everything already on your phone keep working.

What our server keeps

  • A random install identifier created by the app, with counts of how many study-kit pages and questions it has used (for the free allowance and fair use).
  • If you subscribe to Pip Plus: the App Store transaction identifier, product, expiry date and monthly usage counts, so the subscription can be recognised.
  • Daily request counts per hashed network address, to prevent abuse. The address itself isn't stored.

None of this includes your name, email, notes or questions. Our server runs on Supabase.

Subscriptions

Pip Plus is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment; we never see payment details.

Notifications

Study reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No push tokens are sent to us.

Children

Pip is not directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Deleting data

Use “Erase all data” in Pip's Settings to remove everything on your phone. To delete the install or subscription records on our server, email us with the approximate date you installed Pip.
