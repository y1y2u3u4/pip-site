How Pip works

  • Add a lecture PDF and choose the pages your exam covers.
  • Pip builds a study kit: a short guide for each topic, flashcards for key terms and practice questions. Every line is matched word for word against your PDF, and each one links to the exact page.
  • Set your exam date, daily time and study days. Pip plans each day, brings back what you missed, schedules a mock exam and a final look at weak spots, and tells you honestly if the plan doesn't fit.
  • Ask your notes: answers come only from your PDF, with the lines they used.

Common questions

Does Pip need the internet? Building a study kit and Ask your notes do. Everything already built (guides, flashcards, questions, reviews, reminders) works offline.

What's free? The sample notes, one study kit of up to 20 pages from your own PDF, and 5 questions to Ask. Pip Plus adds study kits for all your PDFs, mock exams and more questions (fair use: 300 pages and 100 questions a month).

How do I cancel Pip Plus? Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone, at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A question in my kit is wrong. Tap “Report an issue” under the answer. Pip pauses that question and stops counting it toward your progress.

Scanned PDFs? Pip needs selectable text so it can check every line against your notes. Scanned images aren't supported yet.
